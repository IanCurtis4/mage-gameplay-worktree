## hero

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production pilot sprite for a 2D isometric action RPG, original character. Create ONE compact young adult swordsman adventurer, full body, standing idle facing southeast (three-quarter front right), slight top-down game view. Visual direction: Korean fantasy MMORPG charm from early 2000s, but much more pixelated and simpler, small chibi stature about 2.5 heads tall, expressive dark eyes, fluffy chestnut hair, short teal-blue tunic, simple light steel shoulder guard, brown boots and gloves, short broad sword pointing diagonally down, no shield. True low-detail bitmap pixel art intended for a logical 64x64 cell: body about 44-50 logical pixels tall, selective dark colored outline, chunky coherent square pixel clusters, about 24-32 colors, 3-4 tones per material, warm light from upper left and cool shadows. If output canvas is larger, show a clean nearest-neighbor enlarged 64x64 pixel sprite, not fine high-resolution detail. Place feet at center x and about 88% down the square canvas; generous transparent margin around weapon. Genuinely transparent alpha background, no checkerboard baked in, no ground, no cast shadow, no UI, no lettering, no watermark, no other characters or additional poses. Entire sprite and sword in frame. Avoid smooth gradients, painterly blur, tiny ornamental details, realistic anatomy, and recreating any existing named character.
