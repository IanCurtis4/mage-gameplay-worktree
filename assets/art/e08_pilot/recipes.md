# Fontes raster do piloto E08

Quatro folhas produzidas com ferramenta integrada imagegen em 6/11/2026, a partir das folhas `assets/art/animation_sources/fixes_mobs/animal_companion{,_extra}.png` apenas como referência de estilo e disposição de poses. As fontes novas foram preservadas sem alteração; `provenance.json` registra SHA256 e bounds dos atlas. Nenhum raster compartilhado foi substituído. As solicitações foram:

- Lobo principal: “Create a production source animation sheet for RagRPG, using the supplied image strictly as layout, pose sequence, camera, proportions and painterly pixel-RPG style reference, but replace the character in every cell with the SAME recognizable small forest wolf: quadruped canine, medium pointed ears (not oversized fox ears), long tapered muzzle, charcoal grey and cool silver fur, bushy straight wolf tail, sturdy four paws. Exactly 4 columns by 4 rows, sixteen isolated full-body frames aligned in equal cells with ample transparent margins, no overlap, no text, no grid or drawn checkerboard. Row 1 four front idle variations; row 2 four front walking steps; row 3 four front pouncing/attack steps; row 4 four rear walking steps. Consistent anatomy, palette, scale, feet and identity in every cell. Transparent background. Source sheet to be cut into 64x64 cells; silhouettes must remain legible when tiny.”
- Lobo extra: “First reference specifies the SAME gray forest wolf character, style and proportions. Second reference supplies ONLY the exact 4 by 4 pose layout. Create a matching companion source sheet, transparent background, 16 isolated full-body wolf frames centered in equal cells, no overlap, no lettering, no checkerboard. Row 1 four rear idle frames; row 2 four rear attack/pouncing frames; row 3 two front hurt then two rear hurt frames; row 4 two front fallen/death then two rear fallen/death frames. Wolf anatomy, grey and silver palette, medium pointed ears and bushy tail must match first reference in every cell, and feet remain consistent with its frames. Ensure full figure inside every cell, equivalent scale. No canine replaced by fox, no wounds or gore. Smooth painterly RPG sprite style.”
- Javali principal: “Create a production source animation sheet for RagRPG using the supplied sheet strictly as 4x4 grid, pose ordering, camera and painterly fantasy RPG style reference. Replace all characters with the SAME clearly recognizable wild boar: compact sturdy barrel body, short legs and cloven hooves, broad rounded pig snout, two visible pale upward tusks, small triangular ears, coarse chestnut brown bristles and short tail. This is a boar, never a fox, dog, rabbit, human or giant-eared fantasy companion. Exactly 4 columns by 4 rows, sixteen isolated full-body frames in equal cells with transparent margins, no touching cells, no text, no checkerboard. Row 1 four front idle variations; row 2 four front walking steps; row 3 four front charging/attack steps; row 4 four rear walking steps. Consistent design, scale, camera and paws in every frame. Transparent background. Source will be cut into 64x64 cells; emphasize stout pig silhouette and readable snout/tusks at tiny game size.”
- Javali extra: “First reference is the SAME chestnut brown wild boar character, style and proportions to match exactly: squat barrel body, short legs, small ears, rounded pig snout, ivory tusks, coarse bristles. Second reference supplies ONLY pose ordering in the 4x4 grid. Create matching companion sheet, transparent background, 16 isolated full-body boar frames centered in equal cells with margins, no overlaps, no lettering or checkerboard. Row 1 four rear idle frames; row 2 four rear charging frames; row 3 two front hurt then two rear hurt; row 4 two front fallen/death then two rear fallen/death. Keep consistent boar body and scale across all cells; avoid dog, fox, giant ears or human anatomy, no gore. Painterly fantasy RPG game sprite style matching first reference.”

`tools/prepare_e08_pilot_art.py` reutiliza `prepare_animation_assets.py` para remoção de fragmentos, alinhamento de pé, escala uniforme, quantização e saída 256×512/32 células64×64/pivô(32,58). As folhas são insumos, não prova de legibilidade no renderer. A inspeção visual e os testes da ocorrência são registrados no recibo P3.
