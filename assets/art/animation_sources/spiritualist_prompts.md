Pixel clusters meant for 96x96 game frames. Row1 a small SOUL WISP traveling to the RIGHT: luminous pear-shaped spiritual orb with two small elongated dark eye hollows, long tapering wispy trailing ribbons to LEFT, four flicker phases fixed center, no skull/gore. Row4 WARD / CHANNEL HALO: elliptical isometric ground ring, two small rising wisps at sides and thin vertically flowing ribbons, four breathing animation phases same diameter; deliberately low opacity interior. Every effect fully contained in its own equal cell. Keep rich silhouettes but simple enough for tiny gameplay, restrained glow contained within cell, no characters, no scenery, no typography.
